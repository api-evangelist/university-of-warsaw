The University of Warsaw implements measures to counteract sexual harassment, discrimination, mobbing, and other forms of undesirable behavior. These measures include support for individuals affected by such behavior, procedures for reviewing complaints, training, and educational materials. Students, doctoral candidates, and staff at the University of Warsaw can benefit from the available forms of assistance.
