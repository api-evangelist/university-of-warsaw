The leading academic conference on medieval and Renaissance music was held in Warsaw – at the UW – for the first time. It was attended by 300 musicologists from 26 countries across Europe, Asia, Australia, and South America. The conference showcased the latest research findings in the field of medieval and Renaissance musicology.
