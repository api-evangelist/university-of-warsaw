On 15th September, an agreement was signed at the Ministry of Science and Higher Education, marking the launch of the Polish Press Agency (PAP)’s Scientific Think Tank. The University of Warsaw is one of its partners. The initiative combines the research potential of Polish universities and scientific institutions with the editorial expertise and communication capabilities…
