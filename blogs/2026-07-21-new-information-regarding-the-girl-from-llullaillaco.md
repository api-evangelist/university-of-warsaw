Analysis of plant remains found in the grave of the famous “Doncella of Llullaillaco” has made it possible to determine more precisely when the Inca capacocha ceremony took place. Researchers from the UW have established that the ritual may have been linked not only to religious practices, but also to the consolidation of Inca power…
