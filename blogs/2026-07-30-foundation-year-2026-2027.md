The University of Warsaw has opened the second round of applications for the Foundation Year 2026/2027 programme. Candidates may apply until 19th August. The Foundation Year is a one-year preparatory programme designed for international candidates whose English proficiency is below the B2 level required for admission to English-taught degree programmes.
